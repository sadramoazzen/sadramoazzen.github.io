Computational Neuroscience, NeuroAI, Machine Learning

Welcome! I'm Sadra, a Master's student in Cell and Molecular Biology at Hakim Sabzevari University. My research interests lie at the intersection of computational neuroscience, NeuroAI, machine learning, and neuroimaging.

My research focuses on applying computational and machine learning approaches to biological and neuroscience questions. My research experience includes neural dynamics, sleep-stage decoding, brain connectivity, temporal lobe epilepsy, MRI/fMRI, and machine learning.

I have worked as a researcher with MIT (CompNeuro), the Research Institute of Biotechnology, and Interdisciplinary Schools. My work has resulted in publications and ongoing research in both computational neuroscience and biomedical applications of machine learning.

I am also passionate about education. I have taught and developed courses in computational neuroscience, machine learning, data science, and machine learning for neuroimaging, and have served as a teaching assistant for the Neuromatch Academy.
